# 🇧🇩 Dreams of Bangladesh — Organization Overview

## 🧭 Who We Are

**Dreams of Bangladesh (DOB)** is an innovation-driven organization focused on building a future where technology, creativity, and purpose come together to solve real-world problems.

We are a collective of engineers, thinkers, and builders working toward one goal:
➡️ **Empowering Bangladesh through innovation and engineering**

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

* Develop impactful robotics and engineering solutions
* Inspire the next generation of innovators
* Represent Bangladesh in global technology platforms
* Turn ideas into working, real-world systems

---

## 🔬 What We Do

### 🤖 Robotics Development

We design and build advanced robotic systems for:

* Autonomous navigation
* Environmental monitoring
* Competitive robotics (e.g., WRO)

---

### 🧠 Research & Experimentation

We explore:

* AI-driven systems
* Sensor fusion (LiDAR, IMU, vision)
* Efficient mechanical design

---

### 🌱 Future-Focused Projects

Our work is aligned with real-world challenges:

* Smart agriculture systems
* Climate and environmental solutions
* Space-inspired engineering concepts

---

## 🏗️ Our Approach

We believe in:

* **Build → Test → Improve → Repeat**
* Learning through hands-on engineering
* Designing with purpose, not just complexity
* Creating systems that are scalable and impactful
